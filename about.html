About GMPify

Built by a small team who read the regulations themselves

GMPify exists because pharmaceutical GMP training shouldn't be a paraphrase of a paraphrase. Every course, procedural map and tool is built directly from primary regulatory sources by people who have actually delivered GMP training on the floor of FDA-regulated manufacturing sites.

The Team

GMPify is deliberately small. That means every course is reviewed by someone who understands both the regulation and the platform it lives on, not passed through layers of production staff who never touch the source material.

📚
Lead Instructional Content

27 years of hands-on pharmaceutical GMP training experience shapes every course, procedural map and tool on GMPify. Every piece of content is built directly from primary regulatory sources - 21 CFR, EU GMP, ICH guidelines and current FDA warning letters - not adapted from someone else's summary of them.

How we work

Why a small team builds better GMP training

A large content team means more hands between the regulation and the course. We built GMPify the other way around.

📄

Direct from primary sources

No secondary summaries, no paraphrasing, no AI-generated filler. Every course is built and reviewed by the same person who reads the actual CFR section, ICH guideline or EU GMP Annex.

🔄

Fast to update

When FDA issues new guidance or ICH publishes a revision, a small team can update the catalog in days, not quarters. Our content is reviewed and updated whenever the underlying regulation changes.

✉

You can actually reach us

Every enquiry to training@gmpify.com reaches a real person on this team, not a support queue. Enterprise and custom training requests get a direct reply.
