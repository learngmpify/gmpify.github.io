Change Notification - Reporting Post-Approval Changes to Regulators

A change notification determines how a post-approval manufacturing, formulation, or facility change must be communicated to FDA once an internal change control record has approved the change itself. The regulatory reporting category, Annual Report, CBE-30, CBE-0, or Prior Approval Supplement, is driven entirely by the change's potential impact on product identity, strength, quality, purity, or potency. This map details the pathway from change identification through classification, submission, and implementation.

21 CFR 314.70
Change Reporting Categories (Overview)

Annual Report

Minimal impact changes bundled into the product's next scheduled annual report to FDA.

21 CFR 314.70(d)

CBE-30 / CBE-0

Moderate impact changes effected either 30 days after filing or immediately, depending on urgency and risk.

21 CFR 314.70(c)

Prior Approval Supplement

Substantial impact changes that cannot be implemented until FDA reviews and approves the supplement.

21 CFR 314.70(b)

Change Impact Criteria (Overview)

Minimal potential impact Change has little to no potential to affect the identity, strength, quality, purity, or potency of the product.
Moderate potential impact Change has a moderate potential to affect product quality, but risk is adequately controlled by supporting data.
Substantial potential impact Change has substantial potential to affect product quality, warranting FDA review before implementation.
Classification drives the category The impact level assigned determines whether the change can proceed immediately, after 30 days, or only after approval.

Key Change Notification Outputs (Overview)

Change notification package Complete submission including supporting data, updated CMC content, and category justification.
FDA approval letter Formal approval received for a Prior Approval Supplement before the change may be implemented.
Updated regulatory filing Approved application updated to reflect the implemented change across all relevant CMC sections.
Post-implementation verification Confirmation that the change was implemented as approved and that no unintended quality impact occurred.

Change Notification Quality Elements

Accurate Impact Classification Change consistently classified against the actual potential impact on identity, strength, quality, purity, or potency.
Supporting Data Sufficiency Stability, comparability, or validation data sufficient to support the assigned reporting category.
Cross-Functional Regulatory Review Quality and regulatory affairs jointly review the classification and package before submission.
Timely Submission Notification submitted promptly once supporting data and internal approvals are complete.
Waiting Period Discipline Change implementation withheld until the applicable CBE waiting period or FDA approval is confirmed.
Global Filing Alignment Equivalent notifications or variations filed with other regulators where the same change applies globally.
Post-Implementation Verification Change verified as implemented correctly, with no unintended effect on product quality after the fact.
Documentation Traceability Complete traceability from the internal change control record through to the regulatory submission and approval.

Regulatory Text - Source Provisions

21 CFR 314.70(b)(2) (paraphrased - refer to the official regulation for exact wording)

A change with substantial potential to have an adverse effect on the identity, strength, quality, purity, or potency of the product as they may relate to safety or effectiveness requires FDA approval before the change is implemented.

21 CFR 314.70(c) (paraphrased - refer to the official regulation for exact wording)

A change with moderate potential to have an adverse effect on the product may be made 30 days after FDA receives the supplement, unless FDA notifies the applicant within that time that approval is required first.

21 CFR 314.70(d) (paraphrased - refer to the official regulation for exact wording)

A change with minimal potential to have an adverse effect on the product may be described in the next annual report rather than submitted as an individual supplement.

Best Practice Guidance

Classify with regulatory affairs before generating data Confirm the reporting category early so the supporting data package is scoped correctly the first time.
Calendar the waiting period the day of submission Set a firm implementation date tied to the confirmed submission date, not an estimate made in advance.
Map global filing obligations before implementation Identify every market where the same change requires a notification or variation before the change proceeds anywhere.
Update documentation in the same change record Tie site master file and batch record updates to the same change control record so nothing is updated out of sequence.

Change notification vs change control

Change control is the internal quality system process that evaluates, approves, and implements a change. Change notification is the separate regulatory reporting step that communicates an approved change to FDA, with its own classification and timeline requirements distinct from internal approval.

CBE-30 vs CBE-0 vs PAS

A CBE-30 change may be implemented 30 days after FDA receipt absent an objection. A CBE-0 change may be implemented immediately upon receipt. A Prior Approval Supplement cannot be implemented until FDA affirmatively approves the change.

Key regulations

21 CFR 314.70 - post-approval change reporting requirements. FDA SUPAC Guidance - scale-up and post-approval change classification framework. EU Variations Regulation (EC) 1234/2008 - equivalent EU classification of Type IA, IB, and II variations.
