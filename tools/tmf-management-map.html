TMF Management - Evidence That the Trial Was Conducted in Compliance

The Trial Master File is the collection of essential documents that allows the conduct of a trial and the quality of the resulting data to be evaluated, and confirms compliance with GCP and applicable regulatory requirements. ICH E6(R2) Section 8 defines the essential document categories expected before, during, and after a trial, while the industry TMF Reference Model provides the standard taxonomy most sponsors and CROs build their filing structure around. This map details the pathway from TMF structure and planning through real-time filing, completeness review, and archival.

TMF Ownership and Structure

Trial Master File (Sponsor)

Sponsor-owned collection of trial-wide essential documents, held centrally regardless of how many sites or vendors are involved.

ICH E6(R2) Section 8

Investigator Site File

Site-owned subset of essential documents specific to that site's conduct of the trial, maintained by the investigator.

ICH E6(R2) Section 8

Combined and Vendor-Held TMF

Where a CRO or vendor holds part of the TMF, the sponsor retains ultimate responsibility for its completeness and accuracy.

ICH E6(R2) Section 5.2

Document Quality Standards (Overview)

Legibility Every filed document must be clearly readable, including scanned copies, with no illegible entries or cut-off pages.
Completeness Documents filed in full, including all pages, signatures, dates, and attachments referenced within the document itself.
ALCOA+ principles Filed records remain attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, and accurate, consistent with data integrity expectations across the TMF.
Contemporaneous filing Documents filed at or near the time they are generated, avoiding the retrospective reconstruction risk of batch filing at closeout.

TMF Completeness Metrics (Overview)

Filing timeliness Measures the interval between document generation and filing, flagging documents filed outside the expected window.
Completeness percentage Tracks the proportion of expected documents present against the essential document index at a given point in the trial.
Quality issue rate Tracks documents returned for correction due to legibility, completeness, or misfiling issues, by site and by document type.
Reconciliation status Tracks open discrepancies between the sponsor TMF and investigator site files pending resolution.

TMF Management Quality Elements

TMF Reference Model Structure Filing taxonomy built on a recognized industry standard, enabling consistent structure across trials and easier vendor or CRO alignment.
Real-Time Filing Discipline Documents filed as they are generated rather than reconstructed retrospectively, supporting contemporaneous, defensible records.
Version Control Current and superseded versions of controlled documents clearly distinguished, with the current version always identifiable.
Access Control and Audit Trail Role-based access to the TMF with a complete audit trail of who filed, modified, or approved each document.
Periodic Completeness Reviews Scheduled reviews against the essential document index throughout the trial, not only at closeout.
Site-Sponsor Reconciliation Sponsor TMF and investigator site files periodically compared to confirm consistency and identify gaps early.
Archival Readiness TMF structured and quality-controlled throughout the trial so final archival requires confirmation, not last-minute assembly.
Retention Period Compliance TMF retained in an accessible, unaltered state for the full period required by applicable regulation across all relevant jurisdictions.

Never do this

Wait until trial closeout to assemble the TMF from scratch. File a document without confirming it is legible and complete. Allow superseded and current document versions to be indistinguishable. Destroy or migrate TMF records before the required retention period has elapsed.

TMF vs ISF

The Trial Master File is the sponsor's complete, trial-wide collection of essential documents. The Investigator Site File is the site-specific subset maintained by the investigator. Together they should be reconcilable into one complete record of the trial.

Real-time vs retrospective filing

Real-time filing captures documents as they are generated, supporting contemporaneous, defensible records. Retrospective filing reconstructs the TMF after the fact, increasing the risk of gaps, misdated entries, and inspection findings.

Key regulations

ICH E6(R2) Section 8 - essential documents for the conduct of a clinical trial. TMF Reference Model - standard industry filing taxonomy. EU CTR 536/2014 - EU trial documentation and archival requirements. 21 CFR 312.57 - sponsor recordkeeping and retention.
