Complaints - Every One Evaluated, Every Decision Recorded

A complaint may be the first sign that a device caused harm. Manufacturers need a documented process to receive, evaluate, and investigate complaints, and to decide whether each one is reportable to FDA under the Medical Device Reporting regulation, 21 CFR Part 803. The 30-day reporting clock starts when the manufacturer becomes aware of information that reasonably suggests a reportable event, from any source. This map follows the pathway from receipt and triage through evaluation, reporting, and closure.

Complaint Handling

A documented procedure to receive, review, and evaluate complaints, determine whether to investigate, and decide whether the information is reportable.

Basis: ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.2.2

Reportability Decision

Each complaint is evaluated for MDR reportability, and the decision, including a decision not to report, is documented with its rationale.

Basis: 21 CFR Part 803

Reporting Clock

An initial report is due within 30 calendar days of becoming aware. Events needing remedial action, or requested in writing by FDA, are due within 5 work days.

Basis: 21 CFR 803.10, 803.50

Complaint Records

Records show the device, the dates, the complainant, the nature of the complaint, any investigation, and any action or reply.

Basis: 21 CFR 820.35(a)

Reporting Deadlines (Overview)

5 Work Days

Events requiring remedial action to prevent an unreasonable risk of substantial harm to public health, or events FDA requests in writing.

21 CFR 803.10(c)(2)

30 Calendar Days

Initial report of a death, serious injury, or reportable malfunction, counted from when the manufacturer becomes aware.

21 CFR 803.10(c)(1); 803.50

Supplemental Reports

Submitted when new information is obtained or becomes available after the initial report.

21 CFR 803.56

When Facts Are Missing (Overview)

Do not wait for certainty The decision to report rests on information that reasonably suggests a reportable event, not on proof of cause.
Make follow-up attempts Seek the missing information from the complainant and other sources, and document each attempt.
Report what is known Submit the initial report with the information available and describe what could not be obtained.
Supplement later File a supplemental report when the missing information is obtained.

Complaint and MDR Records (Overview)

Complaint record The device, dates, complainant, and the nature of the complaint.
Evaluation Whether an investigation was made, its results, and the reason if none was made.
Reportability decision The decision, its rationale, and any report submitted.
Action and reply Corrections, corrective actions, and the reply to the complainant.

Complaint and MDR Quality Elements

Single Intake Channel All sources feed one controlled complaint record.
Intake Screening Every complaint is screened for reportability at receipt.
Awareness Date Defined The date the reporting clock starts is recorded.
Documented Decisions Reportable and not reportable decisions both carry a rationale.
Deadline Tracking The 5 work day and 30 day clocks are tracked to submission.
Complete Information Efforts Follow-up for missing information is attempted and recorded.
CAPA and Risk Linkage Confirmed problems feed corrective action and the risk management file.
Trending and Management Visibility Complaint data is trended and reviewed by management.

Never do this

Hold a complaint until the investigation is finished before deciding on reportability. Treat distributor or service reports as outside the clock. Close a not reportable decision with no rationale. Wait for proof of cause before reporting. Send complaints to CAPA only when a customer complains twice.

Awareness and the clock

The reporting clock runs from when the manufacturer becomes aware of information that reasonably suggests a reportable event. Build intake so that awareness is recorded at the moment a complaint arrives, not when the investigation ends.

What changed under the QMSR

Complaint handling now rests on ISO 13485 clause 8.2.2, with record content set by 21 CFR 820.35(a). The Medical Device Reporting regulation, 21 CFR Part 803, is separate and continues to apply.

Key standards

ISO 13485:2016 Clauses 8.2.1 to 8.2.3 - feedback, complaint handling, and reporting to regulatory authorities. 21 CFR 820.35(a) - complaint record content. 21 CFR Part 803 - Medical Device Reporting.
