Nonconforming Product - Contain First, Then Decide

Under the Quality Management System Regulation, the former nonconforming product section, 21 CFR 820.90, is replaced by ISO 13485:2016 clause 8.3, which Part 820 incorporates by reference. Clause 8.3 requires product that does not meet requirements to be identified and controlled to prevent unintended use or delivery, with separate actions for product found before delivery, product found after delivery, and rework. This map follows the pathway from detection and containment through evaluation, disposition, and improvement.

ISO 13485:2016 8.3.1
ISO 13485:2016 8.3.2 to 8.3.4
21 CFR 820.10(a)

Identify and Control

Product that does not conform to requirements is identified and controlled to prevent its unintended use or delivery, under a documented procedure.

Basis: Clause 8.3.1

Before Delivery

Deal with the nonconformity by eliminating it, preventing the original use, or accepting by concession, with records of the concession and who authorized it.

Basis: Clause 8.3.2

After Delivery

Take action appropriate to the effects of the nonconformity. Advisory notice procedures must be capable of being put into effect at any time.

Basis: Clause 8.3.3

Rework

Rework follows a documented procedure that takes into account the potential adverse effect of the rework on the product.

Basis: Clause 8.3.4

Disposition Options (Overview)

Eliminate the Nonconformity

Correct the product so it meets requirements, for example by approved rework with reverification.

ISO 13485:2016 8.3.2, 8.3.4

Preclude Original Use

Prevent the intended use or application, such as by scrapping, returning to the supplier, or redirecting to a different use.

ISO 13485:2016 8.3.2

Accept by Concession

Allowed only with justification, approval, and met regulatory requirements, with records and the authorizing person identified.

ISO 13485:2016 8.3.2

Nonconforming Product Quality Elements

Documented Procedure Identification, documentation, segregation, evaluation, and disposition are defined.
Clear Segregation Nonconforming product cannot be used or delivered by mistake.
Defined Authority Responsibilities and authority for disposition are assigned.
Documented Evaluation The evaluation and any investigation are recorded.
Controlled Concessions Justified, approved, and recorded with the authorizing person.
Controlled Rework Rework is under procedure, with adverse effects considered and reverification.
Post-Delivery Readiness Advisory notice procedures can be put into effect at any time.
Trending and Escalation Data is trended and escalated to CAPA and management review.

Never do this

Move on with product that is waiting for a disposition decision. Accept by concession without justification and approval. Rework without a documented procedure or reverification. Treat a product found after delivery as only a production issue. Close a nonconformance without asking whether the cause needs corrective action.

Containment comes first

Identify and control nonconforming product as soon as it is found. Evaluation and disposition follow, never the other way round.

What changed under the QMSR

The former nonconforming product section, 21 CFR 820.90, is replaced by ISO 13485 clause 8.3. The ISO text separates action before delivery, action after delivery, and rework, and requires records of concessions and advisory notice actions.

Key standards

ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.3.1 - general. Clause 8.3.2 - detected before delivery. Clause 8.3.3 - detected after delivery. Clause 8.3.4 - rework. 21 CFR 820.10(a) and 820.7 - QMS requirement and incorporation of ISO 13485.
