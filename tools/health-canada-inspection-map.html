Health Canada Inspection - Verifying Compliance Under the Food and Drugs Act

A Health Canada inspection verifies that a licensed establishment complies with the Food and Drug Regulations Division 2 requirements, using the GMP expectations set out in guidance document GUI-0001. Observations are classified for risk using the framework in GUI-0023, a three-tier Risk 1, Risk 2, Risk 3 model that determines the site's compliance rating and any impact on its Establishment Licence. This map details the pathway from preparation through conduct, risk classification, and the compliance rating outcome.

Observation Risk Classification (Overview)

Risk 1 (Critical) A practice or process that has produced, or is likely to produce, a product that poses a serious health risk.
Risk 2 (Major) A practice or process that has produced, or may produce, a product that does not meet its required quality standards.
Risk 3 (Other) A practice or process that departs from GMP but does not meet the criteria for a Risk 1 or Risk 2 classification.
Classification drives the rating The number and severity of Risk 1, 2, and 3 observations determines the establishment's overall compliance rating.

Key Inspection Outputs (Overview)

Exit Notification Document provided at the end of the inspection summarizing observations discussed during the exit interview.
Inspection report Formal report listing every observation, its risk classification, and the required corrective action plan timeline.
Compliance rating Overall rating assigned to the establishment based on the number and severity of observations identified.
Establishment Licence status Licence maintained, amended, suspended, or cancelled depending on the compliance rating and response to observations.

Health Canada Inspection Readiness Quality Elements

Continuous GMP Compliance Quality system and facility maintained in ongoing compliance with GUI-0001 expectations, not assembled reactively before an inspection.
Inspection Support Team Readiness Front-facing hosts and subject matter experts identified and briefed on their roles before the inspection begins.
Real-Time Observation Logging Observations raised during the walkthrough and record review logged internally as they occur.
Risk Classification Understanding Site personnel understand the Risk 1, Risk 2, and Risk 3 criteria and their implications for the compliance rating.
Response Timeliness Corrective action plan submitted within the timeline specified in the inspection report.
Root Cause Depth Response addresses the underlying cause of each observation, not only the specific instance the inspector observed.
CAPA Verification Committed corrective actions implemented and their effectiveness verified before the response is considered closed.
Establishment Licence Status Tracking Licence status and any conditions tracked proactively across every activity and site covered by the licence.

Never do this

Treat a Risk 1 observation response with the same urgency as a Risk 3 item. Submit a corrective action plan without addressing root cause. Allow Establishment Licence conditions to go unaddressed. Assume a risk classification without checking the GUI-0023 criteria directly.

Risk 1/2/3 vs EU and FDA models

Health Canada classifies observations as Risk 1, Risk 2, or Risk 3 under GUI-0023. The EU model uses critical, major, and other deficiencies with similar intent. The FDA 483 model lists observations without an equivalent formal severity tier at issuance.

Routine vs for-cause

A routine inspection follows Health Canada's standard risk-based cycle and covers licensed activities broadly. A for-cause inspection responds to a specific concern and is typically narrower but more intensive around that issue.

Key regulations

Food and Drugs Act - statutory basis for Health Canada's inspection authority. Food and Drug Regulations Division 2 - GMP requirements for drugs. GUI-0001 - GMP guidance interpreting Division 2. GUI-0023 - risk classification framework for inspection observations.
