CAPA - Removing the Cause, Not Just the Symptom

Under the Quality Management System Regulation, the old 21 CFR 820.100 CAPA section is replaced by ISO 13485:2016 clause 8.5.2 on corrective action and clause 8.5.3 on preventive action, which Part 820 incorporates by reference. Each clause requires its own documented procedure, with actions proportionate to the effects of the problem. This map follows the pathway from identifying inputs and triage through investigation, action, verification, effectiveness review, and closure.

21 CFR 820.10(a)

Corrective Action

Action to remove the cause of a nonconformity so it does not recur. Needs a documented procedure and records of the investigation and action taken.

Basis: Clause 8.5.2

Preventive Action

Action to remove the cause of a potential nonconformity. Needs its own documented procedure, separate from corrective action.

Basis: Clause 8.5.3

Proportionality

Corrective and preventive actions are proportionate to the effects of the problem encountered or the potential problem.

Basis: Clauses 8.5.2 and 8.5.3

Effectiveness Review

Confirm the action worked, and that it did not adversely affect regulatory compliance or device safety and performance.

Basis: Clause 8.5.2

CAPA Inputs (Overview)

Complaints

Reviewed as nonconformities under the corrective action procedure.

ISO 13485:2016 8.2.2, 8.5.2(a)

Nonconforming Product

Product that does not meet requirements, including product found after delivery, handled according to its effects.

ISO 13485:2016 8.3

Data Analysis, Audits, and Feedback

Trends, internal audit findings, and post-market information that point to an existing or potential problem.

ISO 13485:2016 8.2.1, 8.2.4, 8.4

When Effectiveness Is Not Shown (Overview)

Do not close the record An action that has not been shown to work remains open with a documented next step.
Revisit the cause Check whether the true cause was identified, or whether the investigation stopped at the symptom.
Revise the action Plan and document a further action, and verify it does not adversely affect safety, performance, or compliance.
Re-review effectiveness Apply the same defined criteria again and record the result before closure.

CAPA Records (Overview)

Problem and risk The description, the sources, and the risk-based reason for the type and urgency of action.
Investigation The review, the determined causes, and the evaluation of the need for action.
Action and verification The plan, implementation, updated documents, and the check for adverse effects.
Effectiveness and closure Effectiveness criteria and results, and the documented closure rationale.

CAPA Quality Elements

Separate Corrective and Preventive Procedures Each has its own documented procedure.
Risk-Based Triage Effort and urgency are scaled to the effects of the problem.
Cause Determination Investigation reaches the cause, not only the symptom.
Defined Timelines Action is taken without undue delay, with owners and due dates.
Adverse Effect Check Each action is verified not to harm compliance, safety, or performance.
Effectiveness Criteria Defined Up Front Success measures are set when the action is planned.
Complete Records Investigation results and actions are recorded and retained.
Trending and Management Visibility CAPA data is trended and reviewed by management.

Regulatory Text - Source Provisions

ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.5.2, as incorporated by 21 CFR 820.10(a) (paraphrased - refer to the official regulation for exact wording)

The organization takes action to remove the causes of nonconformities to prevent recurrence, without undue delay and in proportion to the effects. A documented procedure covers reviewing nonconformities including complaints, determining causes, evaluating the need for action, planning and implementing action, verifying the action does not adversely affect regulatory compliance or safety and performance, and reviewing effectiveness. Records of investigation results and actions are kept.

ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.5.3 (paraphrased - refer to the official regulation for exact wording)

The organization determines action to eliminate the causes of potential nonconformities, in proportion to the effects of the potential problems, under a documented procedure that is separate from the one for corrective action.

FDA QMSR presentation on Clause 8.5 (paraphrased - refer to the official regulation for exact wording)

Corrective actions are proportionate to the effects of the nonconformities encountered, and preventive actions are proportionate to the effects of the potential problems. FDA has said that adopting the ISO wording does not change its expectations for preventive action.

Common Inspection Findings - CAPA

Illustrative of recurring themes seen in FDA inspections and audits of device CAPA systems, including under the former Quality System Regulation, which are expected to continue under the QMSR. Not tied to a single specific citation.
No separate preventive action procedure Legacy procedures combine corrective and preventive action in one document without defining the preventive process.
Effectiveness checks too narrow The check only confirms the specific problem did not return and does not look at broader impact.
No evaluation of whether the fix caused new problems No record that the action was checked for adverse effects on compliance, safety, or performance.
Records left open or not prioritized by risk Records open for many months with no documented justification, and minor issues over-investigated while serious ones are under-investigated.

Best Practice Guidance

Set effectiveness criteria when you plan the action Define what success looks like and when it will be checked before the action is implemented.
Use a documented risk-based triage step Record why a problem was treated as a correction, a corrective action, or a preventive action.
Challenge the first root cause Use a second reviewer or a structured method to test whether the cause is truly the cause.
Review aging records at a fixed interval Review all open records on a schedule and document the reason for any that stay open.

Never do this

Close a record because the task was completed, not because the action worked. Stop at the first plausible cause. Keep corrective and preventive action in one undefined procedure. Skip the check for adverse effects. Leave a record open for months with no documented rationale.

Corrective vs preventive

Corrective action removes the cause of a nonconformity that has occurred so it does not recur. Preventive action removes the cause of a potential nonconformity. ISO 13485 requires a documented procedure for each, and actions proportionate to the effects.

What changed under the QMSR

The combined CAPA section of the former regulation, 21 CFR 820.100, is replaced by ISO 13485 clauses 8.5.2 and 8.5.3. The ISO text adds an explicit check that the action does not adversely affect regulatory compliance or safety and performance, and an effectiveness review.

Key standards

ISO 13485:2016 Clause 8.5.2 - corrective action. Clause 8.5.3 - preventive action. Clauses 8.2.2, 8.3, 8.4 - complaints, nonconforming product, and analysis of data. 21 CFR 820.10(a) and 820.7 - QMS requirement and incorporation of ISO 13485.
