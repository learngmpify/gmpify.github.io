Food manufacturing courses

Nine courses covering the requirements FDA applies to food manufacturers, importers and their suppliers. More food and dietary supplement courses are being added through Q1 2027.

Foundation

Food GMP Essentials for Production Employees

The practical rules behind 21 CFR 117.4 for the people who make, pack and handle food every day.

Preventive Controls

Chemical Hazards and Preventive Controls in Food Manufacturing

How a facility identifies, evaluates and controls chemical hazards under 21 CFR Part 117, so a problem is caught before it reaches the product.

Allergens

Allergen Control and Labeling Compliance

Preventing undeclared allergens through cross-contact controls, accurate labeling and the written allergen control plan.

Sanitation

Food Facility Sanitation and Hygiene

Cleanliness in a food facility is not a single rule, it is a system.

Microbiology

Environmental Monitoring and Listeria Control for RTE Facilities

Why Listeria monocytogenes is one of the most serious hazards for ready-to-eat producers, and how a facility keeps it under control.

Supply Chain

Supplier Verification Programs

When a hazard is controlled upstream by the supplier, Subpart G of 21 CFR Part 117 requires a supply-chain program.

Importers

Foreign Supplier Verification Programs (FSVP) for Food Importers

How importers verify that foreign suppliers produce food to U.S. safety standards, under 21 CFR Part 1, Subpart L.

Food Defense

Food Defense and Intentional Adulteration

Preparing for deliberate contamination under the intentional adulteration rule, 21 CFR Part 121.

Recall

Recall Management and Traceability

What a recall is, how FDA classifies it, and what to have in place before one starts.
