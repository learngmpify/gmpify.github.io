Essentials Series

QC Lab Testing Fundamentals

How a sample moves from receipt to result, why a receiving lab must requalify a transferred method, and where OOS handling begins.

What this course covers

This course covers how a sample moves through a QC lab from receipt to reportable result and the documentation required at each step, method transfer basics and why a receiving lab must requalify a method rather than simply adopt it, the role QC testing plays in the batch release decision, and an introduction to OOS handling and when a result needs to be escalated to a full investigation.

Who this course is for

  • New QC laboratory hires
  • Lab supervisors standardizing onboarding across a team
  • Cross-functional staff who work with QC data without a lab background

What you'll learn

  • How samples move through a QC lab from receipt to result, and the documentation at each step
  • Method transfer basics and why a receiving lab must requalify a method, not just use it
  • The role of QC testing in the batch release decision
  • An introduction to OOS handling and when to escalate to the full OOS investigation process
  • Common QC lab data integrity risks specific to the testing environment

Why it matters

A surprising number of QC data integrity findings trace back to gaps in basic lab workflow discipline, an unlogged sample, an undocumented method deviation, rather than deliberate misconduct. Getting the fundamentals right prevents most of what later becomes a serious finding.
