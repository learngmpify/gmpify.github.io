Essentials Series

Pharmaceutical Quality Essentials

How deviation management, CAPA, change control and APQR function together as one integrated quality system, not separate silos.

What this course covers

This course ties together deviation management, CAPA, change control and the annual product quality review into a single integrated view of how a pharmaceutical quality system actually functions, using the ICH Q10 pharmaceutical quality system model as the organizing framework. It is built for anyone who needs the big picture across a quality system without deep-diving into every individual process first.

Who this course is for

  • New QA hires building a foundational understanding of the full quality system
  • Cross-functional staff and managers who need the big picture, not depth in every process

What you'll learn

  • How deviation management, CAPA, change control and APQR function together as one integrated quality system, not separate silos
  • The pharmaceutical quality system model from ICH Q10 as the organizing framework
  • Where each individual quality process feeds data to the others
  • How a single quality event can move through investigation, CAPA and eventually into an APQR trend
  • When to go deeper into GMPify's dedicated courses on each individual topic

Why it matters

Quality processes are often taught and documented as separate procedures, which obscures how tightly they actually depend on each other. Seeing the whole system in one course, before specializing, makes every individual deep-dive course make more sense.
