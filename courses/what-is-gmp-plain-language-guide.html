What Is GMP? A Plain Language Guide

What GMP actually means, how it's enforced across FDA, EMA, MHRA and WHO, and the patient-safety principle underneath every specific requirement.

What this course covers

This course is a broad, plain-language introduction to Good Manufacturing Practice for anyone in the pharmaceutical industry, not only manufacturing floor staff. It covers the patient-safety principle underneath every specific GMP requirement, how GMP is enforced differently across FDA, EMA, MHRA and WHO, and how GMP relates to broader quality frameworks like ISO. It closes with the real-world consequences of non-compliance, from a 483 observation through to a consent decree.

Who this course is for

  • Anyone new to the pharmaceutical industry in any role, not only manufacturing
  • Business, commercial or IT staff who need GMP literacy to work effectively with quality teams
  • Anyone who wants a conceptual foundation before diving into specific regulations

What you'll learn

  • What GMP means and the patient-safety principle underneath every specific requirement
  • How GMP is enforced differently across FDA, EMA, MHRA and WHO
  • The relationship between GMP, the broader GxP family, and frameworks like ISO
  • Real consequences of non-compliance, from a 483 observation through to a consent decree
  • How to read and navigate a regulation without a legal or scientific background

Why it matters

A surprising number of people who work adjacent to a regulated pharmaceutical operation, sales, finance, IT, never receive a real explanation of why GMP exists or what it actually requires. That gap creates friction with quality teams and slows decisions that touch compliance.
