Annual Product Quality Review (APQR)

What FDA's 211.180(e) and EU GMP's Product Quality Review actually require, and what data must be trended rather than just tabulated.

What this course covers

This course covers FDA's annual product review requirement under 211.180(e) and EU GMP's Product Quality Review under Chapter 1, what data must be genuinely trended across a review period rather than simply tabulated, and how an APQR's conclusions should feed back into CAPA, validation status and specifications rather than being filed and forgotten.

Who this course is for

  • QA staff compiling or reviewing annual product reviews
  • Site leadership using APQR conclusions to prioritize improvement work

What you'll learn

  • FDA's annual product review requirement under 211.180(e) and EU GMP's Product Quality Review under Chapter 1
  • What data must be trended across a review period, not just tabulated
  • How an APQR should feed conclusions back into CAPA, validation status and specifications
  • The difference between a review that satisfies the letter of the requirement and one that drives improvement
  • Common APQR gaps found during inspections

Why it matters

An APQR that restates the year's data without drawing conclusions is a common and avoidable finding. Trended data, especially a pattern like a cluster of closed-with-no-root-cause investigations for the same product, is exactly the kind of signal an APQR exists to surface.
