21 CFR Part 211 Module 4: Production & Packaging

Production and process controls under Subpart F, and packaging and labeling controls under Subpart G.

What this course covers

This module covers Subpart F production and process controls, written procedures, component charge-in, yield calculation, equipment identification, microbiological contamination control and reprocessing, alongside Subpart G packaging and labeling requirements: labeling issuance, control and reconciliation, packaging operations, tamper-evident packaging and drug product inspection and expiration dating.

Who this course is for

  • Production supervisors and line staff
  • Packaging and labeling control coordinators
  • QA staff reviewing batch and packaging records

What you'll learn

  • Written procedure and process control requirements under Subpart F
  • Component charge-in and yield calculation documentation expectations
  • Microbiological contamination control and reprocessing requirements
  • Labeling issuance, control and reconciliation requirements under Subpart G
  • Tamper-evident packaging and expiration dating requirements

Why it matters

Labeling mix-ups and yield discrepancies are two of the most consequential errors a production floor can make, since one leads directly to patient safety risk and the other is often the first sign that something in the process went wrong unnoticed.
