Pharmaceutical Water Systems Essentials

Water generation methods, storage and distribution loop design, and the validation lifecycle from design qualification through ongoing monitoring.

What this course covers

This course covers water system design principles including generation, storage and distribution loop design, common water generation methods such as distillation and reverse osmosis and how to choose between them, the validation lifecycle for a pharmaceutical water system from design qualification through ongoing monitoring, and the chemical and microbial quality attributes a water system must control.

Who this course is for

  • Engineers and facilities staff new to pharmaceutical water systems
  • QA staff who need a design-level understanding before specializing in water system microbiology

What you'll learn

  • Water system design principles including generation, storage and distribution loop design
  • Common water generation methods such as distillation and reverse osmosis, and how to choose between them
  • The validation lifecycle for a pharmaceutical water system, from design qualification through ongoing monitoring
  • Chemical and microbial quality attributes a water system must control
  • How water system essentials connect to the deeper microbiology-focused water systems course

Why it matters

Water system problems are expensive to fix after construction, since the piping, loop design and materials of construction are hard to change once installed. Understanding the design principles before a system is built or renovated is where this course adds the most value.
