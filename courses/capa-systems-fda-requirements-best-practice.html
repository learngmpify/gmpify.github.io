Quality Systems

CAPA Systems: FDA Requirements & Best Practice

The four citation patterns behind most CAPA-related warning letters, and what an effectiveness check actually needs to demonstrate.

What this course covers

This course covers FDA's expectations for a CAPA system, the citation patterns most often found in CAPA-related warning letters, and what an effectiveness check actually needs to demonstrate, not just confirm that an action happened. It also covers how CAPA differs from a simple corrective action closed at the source, and how to build a CAPA program that improves over time instead of accumulating open items.

Who this course is for

  • QA staff who own or manage a site's CAPA system
  • Site quality leadership responsible for CAPA program health
  • Anyone writing or approving CAPA effectiveness checks

What you'll learn

  • FDA's expectations for a CAPA system under quality system regulation principles
  • The citation patterns most often found in CAPA-related FDA warning letters
  • What an effectiveness check actually needs to demonstrate, not just confirm the action was taken
  • How CAPA differs from a simple corrective action closed at the source
  • Building a CAPA program that improves over time instead of accumulating open items

Why it matters

CAPA deficiencies were cited in 26 of 44 FDA drug warning letters in FY2025, making it one of the single most common warning letter themes. A CAPA system that closes items without verifying they actually worked is not a quality system, it is a documentation exercise.
